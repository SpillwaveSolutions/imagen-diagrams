# imagen-diagrams

This plugin converts Mermaid and PlantUML into slide and article figures.

- Author skills stay outside: design-doc-mermaid, plantuml.
- Salt wireframes never go through Imagen. Use the plantuml JAR.
- Production prompt is six-block layout-hints. See skills/imagen-diagrams/references/prompt-contract.md.
- Brace policy is per backend. Never copy the wrong escape.
- Backend auto: imagen, then grok, then codex.
- Themes are YAML. Do not hard-code palettes in SKILL.md.
- Publication rules: skills/imagen-diagrams/references/publication-contract.md.
- Zero em dash characters in prompts, captions, labels, and generated copy.
