# Publication contract (skill pointer)

The full contract lives in the product PRD section 10. This file is the skill-facing summary.

## Deliverables

Article, companion deck, website, designed figures, table figures where useful, glossary, one visual system, one terminology system.

## Forbidden in the finished publication

- raw Mermaid or PlantUML as the figure
- ASCII diagrams
- invented or dropped nodes
- unexplained figures or tables
- inconsistent component names
- undefined uncommon terms
- fabricated technical detail
- em dash characters

## Diagram workflow

1. Understand the concept.
2. Define semantics (Mermaid or PlantUML may stay internal).
3. Editorial simplify. Slide density is stronger than article density.
4. Generate the illustration (or accuracy-fallback SVG).
5. Validate against the inventory.
6. Explain the figure in one or two paragraphs.

## Visual reference

Use the visual language of https://spillwave.com/guides/ as a style reference. Extract principles. Do not copy assets.

Default palettes: claude-clay for screen and slides, manning-print for book figures.
